What to Wear

We invite our beloved guests to celebrate with us dressed in Any Shade of Brown. Think warm earthy tones, rich espresso, caramel, soft latte, taupe, and bronze.

For Ladies: Dress

Elegant dresses in any shade of brown—from long floor-length gowns, cocktail dresses, to midi dresses. Choose rich silk, chiffon, linen, or satin in tan, mocha, caramel, or deep chocolate tones.

Styling Tip: Complement with warm gold accessories, nude heels, or subtle floral accents!

For Gentlemen: Long Sleeves

Formal long-sleeve dress shirts, Barong Tagalog with warm undertones, or long-sleeve button-downs paired with tailored trousers in beige, camel, mocha, or dark espresso brown.

Styling Tip: Dark brown leather shoes, matching belt, and neatly rolled long sleeves or suit jackets!
✓ Please Wear: Any shade of brown, beige, tan, mocha, camel, or terracotta dresses & long-sleeve attire.
✕ Please Avoid: Pure bridal white, neon colors, short sleeves, or casual denim jeans.
